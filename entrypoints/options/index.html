Learned rules

Drag a tab into another site's group, for example a Grafana tab into the Atlassian group. Tab Tidy remembers it, and later tidies put that site's tabs in that group. Drag a tab back to its own site's group to forget the rule.

    Privacy

    • Without an API key, nothing leaves the browser.
    • With a key, only tab titles, site paths and group names go to Claude. Query strings and #fragments are removed first.
    • Requests go straight from your browser to api.anthropic.com. There is no Tab Tidy server.
    • Your key and your rules are stored only in this browser profile. They are not synced.
    • Pinned tabs and tabs in a call or playing audio are never grouped, closed, or put to sleep.
    • Saved sessions stay on this device.
